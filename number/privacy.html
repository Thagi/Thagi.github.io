Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Number handles information. Number is a single-player puzzle game.

Information stored on device

Number stores gameplay progress, settings, language selection, sound settings, and best results locally on your device. This information is used only to operate the app experience.

Advertising

Number uses Google AdMob to display banner advertisements. Google and its partners may collect and use device identifiers, approximate location, ad interaction data, diagnostics, and other information as described in Google's policies.

Google's privacy information is available at policies.google.com/privacy.

Tracking and consent

Number does not require an account and does not ask you to provide personal information directly in the app. If a platform consent or tracking prompt is shown in a future version, your choice will control the related ad personalization behavior where applicable.

Children

Number is not designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a third-party advertising service, please contact that service provider and use the support page for this app.

Contact

For support or privacy questions, use the Number support page.
