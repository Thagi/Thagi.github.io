Color Support

Color is a single-player ice-sliding puzzle game. If the app does not behave as expected, try the steps below first.

Basic checks

  • Restart the app.
  • Confirm that your device is running the latest available iOS version.
  • Check the in-app settings for language, sound effects, and background music.
  • If audio does not play, confirm that silent mode is off and volume is up.

Ads

Banner ads may not always appear. This can happen when there is no available ad inventory, during network issues, or while AdMob is preparing a new app or ad unit.
